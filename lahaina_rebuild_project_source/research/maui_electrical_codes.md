# Maui County Electrical Codes Research

## National Electrical Code Adoption

Maui County has adopted the "NFPA 70, National Electrical Code, 2008 Edition" as the basis for their electrical code. This was prepared by the National Electrical Code Committee and issued by the Standards Council on July 26, 2007, with an effective date of August 15, 2007.

The code is incorporated by reference and made a part of the Maui County Code, subject to amendments set forth in Chapter 16.18B.

## Key Electrical Code Requirements

1. **Permits Required**: Electrical permits are required for new electrical installations, with some exceptions for minor work.

2. **Minimum Standards**: The purpose of the code is to reduce hazards to persons and property arising from faulty electrical work. The requirements provide minimum standards for electrical work in the County.

3. **Enforcement**: The director of public works administers and enforces the provisions of the code.

4. **Inspections**: Electrical installations must be inspected to ensure compliance with the code.

5. **Compliance with Building and Safety Codes**: All electrical work must comply with applicable building and safety codes (Title 16).

## Rebuilding After Wildfires

### Ordinance 5780 (Effective March 24, 2025)

This ordinance allows property owners to rebuild nonconforming structures lost in the August 2023 wildfires, provided they meet specific conditions:

1. **Timeline**: Property owners have until April 1, 2029, to obtain a building permit, complete reconstruction, and secure final inspection approval.

2. **Requirements**:
   - Provide evidence that the structure was legally nonconforming and existed before the wildfires
   - Ensure the repair or reconstruction does not increase the structure's nonconformity
   - Complete all work in compliance with applicable building and safety codes (Title 16)
   - Meet all deadlines, including final inspection approval by April 1, 2029
   - Comply with Shoreline Rules, if applicable

3. **Extensions**: A two-year extension may be granted for properties located in a historic district or Special Management Area, provided good cause is demonstrated.

## Implications for Electrical Services Website

1. The website must ensure all electrical service estimates and plans comply with the 2008 National Electrical Code as adopted by Maui County, with local amendments.

2. The AI component should incorporate these code requirements when generating estimates and plans.

3. The system should be aware of the special provisions for rebuilding after the 2023 wildfires, including the timeline and requirements under Ordinance 5780.

4. The website should provide information about permit requirements and the process for obtaining electrical permits in Maui County.
